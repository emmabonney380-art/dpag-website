About DPAG

Diaspora Project Assist Ghana (DPAG) was established to bridge the gap between clients living abroad and their projects, properties, investments, and activities in Ghana. We understand the unique challenges faced by diaspora members who cannot be physically present to oversee their interests on the ground.

As your trusted local representative, DPAG provides comprehensive project assistance and professional representation services that give you peace of mind, transparency, and confidence in managing your affairs in Ghana—no matter where you are in the world.
